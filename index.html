Company Profile

Powder coating products for modern industrial applications

ROHIT POWDER COAT AND CHEMICALS PRIVATE LIMITED offers a range of powder coating materials and solutions designed to protect and enhance metal substrates. Our product range includes epoxy, epoxy polyester / hybrid, pure polyester and super durable polyester formulations to suit different finishing requirements, application needs and environmental conditions.

These powder coating systems are selected to deliver specific performance attributes such as corrosion resistance, toughness, flexibility, durability, color retention and finish variety, with products suitable for indoor and outdoor applications.

Powder Coating

What is Powder Coating?

Powder coating is a dry coating, applied as a free-flowing dry powder. It does not require a solvent to keep the binder or filler in liquid suspension. The powder is applied electrostatically and then cured under heat, creating a hard and smooth finish. It is mainly used for metal substrates.

01

Surface Preparation

Phosphating, chromotising and sand blasting as applicable.

02

Powder Application

Electrostatic application of powder to the grounded metal substrate.

03

Curing

The coated part is placed into a curing oven.

04

Finished Coating

A hard and smooth protective finish is formed.
